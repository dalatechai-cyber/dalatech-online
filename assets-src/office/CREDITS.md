# Office page art credits

Source art for the /office page lives here and is turned into
`public/office/pack.png` + `src/office/pack.json` by
`scripts/build-office-pack.py`. The site ships only the built atlas.

## pixel-agents (`pixel-agents/`)
Furniture, floors and character sheets from
https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents — MIT License (see
`pixel-agents/LICENSE`). Their characters are based on JIK-A-4's
"MetroCity – Free Top Down Character Pack" (CC0):
https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metrocity-free-topdown-character-pack

Colours are shifted toward the site's navy in the build script; the
originals are kept unmodified in this folder.

The site footer carries the credit line for both.
