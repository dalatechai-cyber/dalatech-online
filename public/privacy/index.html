Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Dalatech collects when you contact us — through our Facebook Page on Messenger, or by sending a demo request from dalatech.online — why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have over your data.

Last updated: 8 May 2026

1. Who we are

Dalatech is a tech agency based in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, owned and operated by Tserentsoodol Bilguun. We build websites, AI chatbots, and business automation for small and mid-size businesses.

For any privacy question, you can reach us at dalatech.ai@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect through Facebook Messenger

When you message our Facebook Page, we receive and store the following information from Meta’s Messenger Platform:

  • The content of the messages you send to us, and our replies.
  • Your Facebook user ID (a numeric identifier scoped to our Page).
  • Your public profile name and profile picture as shown on Facebook.
  • The timestamp and language of each message, plus any attachments (images, files, audio) you choose to send us.

We do not ask for, collect, or store your password, payment card numbers, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data through Messenger.

3. What data we collect through the demo request form

When you send a demo request from dalatech.online, we receive only what you type into that form:

  • Your name.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your business name and what your business does.
  • Which of our services you selected, if any.
  • Any note you chose to add, and your email address if you gave one.
  • The page you sent it from, the language the site was in, and the date and time — recorded automatically so we know the context of your enquiry.

The form has no account, no tracking pixel and no advertising cookie. We do not ask for, collect, or store your password, payment card numbers, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data through it.

4. Why we collect this data

We use the data above for one purpose only: to read and respond to the enquiries you send us. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Reply to your questions about our services and pricing.
  • Continue an ongoing conversation across multiple messages.
  • Call you back about a demo request you sent us, at the number you gave us for that purpose.
  • Send a follow-up reply within Facebook’s allowed messaging window if you previously contacted us.

We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not use these details for marketing you did not ask for.

5. How long we keep it

We retain Messenger conversations for up to 24 months from the date of your last message, after which they are deleted from our records. Demo requests are kept for up to 24 months from the date you sent them, on the same basis. You may ask us to delete either sooner at any time (see Section 8).

6. Sharing — we do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. We do not use your Messenger data for advertising or to build profiles about you.

The only parties with incidental access to your data are:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — because the messages travel through Facebook Messenger’s infrastructure under Meta’s own privacy terms.
  • Authorized Dalatech staff who handle customer support, who are bound by confidentiality.

We may disclose data only if compelled by valid Mongolian law (for example, a court order).

7. How we protect it

Conversations are accessed through Facebook’s Page Inbox and any tooling we connect via the official Messenger Platform API. Access is limited to authorized personnel, protected by Facebook authentication, and we follow standard practices such as two-factor authentication on accounts that can read Page messages.

8. Your rights — access, correction, deletion

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • Tell you what data we hold about you from Messenger.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your conversation history with our Page.

To make any of these requests, email dalatech.ai@gmail.com from the email associated with your Facebook account, or reply on Messenger asking us to delete your data. We will action the request within 30 days.

For full deletion instructions, see our Data Deletion page.

9. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has contacted us, email us and we will delete the conversation.

10. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be announced on our Facebook Page.

Questions?
Email dalatech.ai@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
