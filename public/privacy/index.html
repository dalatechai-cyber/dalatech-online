2. Data collected through Facebook, Instagram and website messages

When you message our Facebook Page on Messenger, we receive and store the following from Meta's Messenger Platform:

  • The content of the messages you send us, and our replies.
  • Your Facebook user ID — a numeric identifier scoped to our Page.
  • Your public profile name and profile picture as shown on Facebook.
  • The timestamp and language of each message, and any attachments you send (images, files, audio).

When Dali answers on a client's Facebook Page, Instagram account or website, the same kinds of data — message content, user ID, public profile name, timestamps and attachments — are processed on that client's behalf. Those conversations belong to the client and the client's staff can read them.

Dali takes bookings and appointments. Details from a conversation, such as a name, a phone number and the time booked, can therefore remain in that business's records.

To produce a reply, the text of a message is sent to an AI model provider. This means your message is processed not only by us but also by the service that generates the answer. Section 8 names who that is.

Dali is an AI staff member, not a person, and does not present itself as a human. When it cannot answer a question, it hands the conversation to a human member of the client's staff, who then reads the conversation history. You can ask to speak to a person at any time.

Dali cannot conclude contracts, take payments, or make credit or eligibility decisions. An AI can also answer wrongly. Its answers are not binding commitments until the business itself confirms them.

Vira (analyst) is not yet in service. Once it is, it will produce its monthly report from that business's own messages, bookings and sales records. The report goes to that business only.

Support staff at DalaTech look at a conversation only to investigate a fault or at the client's request. We do not use conversation content in advertising, examples or portfolio material, and we do not show it to other clients.

The chat window on our own website is loaded from a separate address (dalatech-chatbot.vercel.app). Messages sent there are processed in the same way as Messenger conversations.

Eho (phone operator) and Nova (customer care) are not yet in service. We therefore receive no calls, recordings, transcripts or caller numbers. Nova's follow-up messages and reminders are not being sent yet. Before either goes into service we will update this policy and set out exactly how voice data and follow-up messages are handled.

Ora (personal assistant) is not in service either. We therefore receive and hold no documents or correspondence through Ora. Before Ora goes into service we will update this policy and set out exactly how the contracts and documents given to it are stored and processed.

We do not ask for, collect or store passwords, payment card numbers, government ID details or any other sensitive personal data through messages.

Each kind of data is collected for a stated purpose:

  • Messages and demo requests sent to us: to answer your questions, quote a price, call you back on the number you gave us, and follow the thread of an ongoing conversation.
