Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains, step by step, how to ask DalaTech to delete the data we hold about you, what to put in the request, and how long we take. There is no charge for a request.

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. Who this page is for

Any of the following people can ask us to delete the data we hold about them:

  • Anyone who has messaged the DalaTech Facebook Page through Messenger.
  • Anyone who has chatted with us through the chat window on dalatech.online.
  • Anyone who has sent a demo request or a pre-registration request from the site.
  • Anyone who has chatted with Dali, DalaTech's AI staff member, on another business's Facebook, Instagram or website — see section 7.
  • A business that buys our services — see section 8.

2. What you can ask us to delete

If you contacted us through Messenger, we hold:

  • The messages you sent us and our replies.
  • Your Facebook user ID — a numeric identifier that applies only to our Page.
  • Your public display name and profile picture on Facebook.
  • The date, time and language of each message, and any attachment you sent (image, file, audio).

If you wrote through the chat window on our site, we hold your messages, our replies, and the date and time. No Facebook profile data reaches us through that channel — we know your name or phone number only if you typed it into the chat yourself.

If you sent a demo request or a pre-registration request, we hold:

  • Your name, phone number, business name, and your description of what your business does.
  • The service you selected, any note you added, and your email address if you gave one.
  • The page you sent it from, the language the site was in, and the date and time.

Eho, the phone operator, and Nova, customer care, are not yet in service. We therefore hold no call recordings and no voice data. What you give us when you pre-register for them is the same data as the demo request listed above.

3. How to send the request

There are two routes, and both carry the same weight. We do not run a self-serve form — a person reads the request, finds the right conversation, deletes it, and confirms back to you.

  • Email: write to dalatech.ai@gmail.com with the subject line "Data deletion request — DalaTech".
  • Facebook: message the DalaTech Facebook Page on Messenger and tell us you want your data deleted.

Where you can, send the email from the address linked to your Facebook account — that lets us verify the request quickly. If you cannot, message us on Messenger from the same Facebook account you used before.

4. What to put in the request

So that we find the right data, please tell us:

  • For Messenger: your Facebook display name, and the approximate date you last wrote to us.
  • For the chat window on our site: the approximate date and time you chatted, and the name or phone number if you left one in the chat.
  • For a demo or pre-registration request: the name and phone number you entered in it.
  • If you chatted with an AI staff member on another business's page: the name of that business, the channel you used (Facebook, Instagram, website), and the approximate date.

We do not need any other personal detail. If we cannot find the conversation, or we need to confirm that the request is really yours, we will ask one further question. We will not delete someone else's data on an unverified request.

5. What we do, and how long it takes

  • We confirm by email within 3 business days that we have received the request.
  • If we need to, we ask one clarifying question so we delete the right conversation.
  • We delete your data from the Page inbox, from the systems connected to it, from the site chat records, from our demo request records, and from the copies of the email and messaging notifications we received about that request. Any notes we made about the conversation are deleted with it.
  • We confirm by email once it is done. The whole process is completed within 30 days of your first request.

Making a request costs nothing, and neither does carrying it out.

6. What is deleted, and what remains

Once the request is carried out, the data listed in section 2 is no longer in our systems. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

The following is not deleted:

  • The copy of the conversation in your own Facebook or Messenger account. You can delete that yourself from the Messenger app at any time.
  • Data held inside Meta's own systems, which is governed by Meta's privacy terms.
  • Any copy held by the AI model provider used to draft replies. Messages pass through that provider so an answer can be generated, and what remains on their side is governed by their own terms. Our Privacy Policy names who the messages pass through.
  • Technical server logs that automatically record a request sent from the site. These logs are kept for a short time and are overwritten automatically, so they cannot be deleted individually.
  • A minimal record of the deletion request itself: the date it arrived and the date it was completed. This record holds no message content and we use it only to show that the request was carried out.
  • If you have paid for our services, the payment and accounting records that law requires us to keep.

7. If you chatted with an AI staff member of another business

AI staff such as Dali work on behalf of our client businesses, on their Facebook, Instagram and website channels. Data from those conversations belongs to that business; DalaTech only stores and processes it on their instructions.

  • The fastest route is to contact that business directly and ask them to delete your data.
  • Alternatively, write to us. We pass the request to the business and, on their instruction, delete the data from the systems we run for them.

The timing is the same: we acknowledge within 3 business days and complete within 30 days. Anything that business keeps in its own records can only be deleted by them.

8. For businesses that buy our services

If you buy an AI staff member or a website from DalaTech, you can ask us to delete:

  • The business information you gave us to train the AI staff member — prices, services, common questions, documents: the knowledge base.
  • The conversation history with your customers that we hold on your behalf, and the reports prepared from it.
  • The access you granted to your Facebook Page. You can also revoke that yourself at any time in your Facebook settings.

Send the request by email. We acknowledge it within 3 business days. If you want a copy of your conversation history and knowledge base before we delete them, tell us and we will provide it. We complete the deletion within 30 days of the request and confirm it by email. Payment records that law requires us to keep are not covered by this.
