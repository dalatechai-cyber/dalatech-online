Data Deletion Instructions

If you have ever messaged the Dalatech Facebook Page through Messenger, you can ask us to delete the data we hold about you. This page explains exactly how.

Last updated: 8 May 2026

What data this covers

Through Facebook Messenger, Dalatech receives and stores a limited set of information from Meta’s Messenger Platform:

  • The messages you sent to our Page and our replies.
  • Your Facebook user ID (a numeric identifier scoped to our Page).
  • Your public profile name and profile picture.
  • Timestamps and any attachments you sent.

A deletion request will remove all of the above from our records. For full context on how this data is collected and used, see our Privacy Policy.

How to request deletion

We do not run a self-serve deletion form — deletion requests are handled by a human at Dalatech to make sure we delete the right conversation and confirm it back to you.

  1. Send an email to dalatech.ai@gmail.com with the subject line “Data deletion request — Dalatech Facebook Messenger”.
  2. In the email, include the Facebook display name you used when messaging our Page, and the approximate date of your last message so we can locate the conversation. You do not need to share any other personal details.
  3. Send the email from the address associated with your Facebook account where possible — this lets us verify the request quickly. If that is not possible, reply to us on Messenger from the same Facebook account asking us to delete your data.

→ dalatech.ai@gmail.com

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your request by email within 3 business days.
  2. We delete the conversation and any related notes from our Page inbox and any tooling we have connected to the Messenger Platform API.
  3. We confirm completion to you by email. The full process is handled within 30 days of your initial request.

Things to know

  • Deletion is irreversible — once we delete your conversation, we cannot recover it.
  • Deletion only affects what Dalatech stores. It does not delete the conversation from your own Facebook account; you can do that from your Messenger app at any time.
  • We may retain a minimal record of the deletion request itself (date and the fact that it was completed) for our own audit purposes, with no message content.
Need help with the request?
Email dalatech.ai@gmail.com and a person at Dalatech will walk you through it. We respond within 30 days, usually within 3 business days.
