Ten logo concepts, side by side

Three refinements of the current wave-D (01–03), four new directions (04–07, three of them not letters at all) and three wordmarks (08–10). Every mark is hand-built geometry exported as a single flat SVG fill, so each one sits cleanly on any background. Nothing here is on the live site.

Navy #060E24 Sky #60C8FF on dark Deep sky #0B73C2 on light Brand blue #2563EB 2nd tone on dark Mist #F5F8FF text on dark

Colour rules. Light blue #60C8FF and dark blue #060E24 carry every concept. One-colour marks switch to Deep sky #0B73C2 on white, because #60C8FF alone reaches only 1.9:1 contrast there and washes out; Deep sky is the same hue at 5:1. Two-tone marks (05–07) pair light blue with navy on light grounds, and with brand blue #2563EB on dark grounds, where navy would vanish.

Notes

How the marks are made. Circles, rectangles, arcs and a few Bézier curves on a 96-unit grid, merged with boolean operations into one path per colour. No traced raster, gradients, shadows or effects. Wordmark type is outlined from Manrope (SIL Open Font License, which allows logo use); the 10 letters are drawn from scratch.

Resemblance checks were done by eye against well-known tech and finance marks, not a trademark search. Run a proper search in Mongolia (and wherever you register) on the finalist before committing.
